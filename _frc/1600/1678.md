FRC Team 1678, Citrus Circuits, is a team from Davis, California. 1678 consists of more than 100 students as of 2026. Students are grades 9-12 from Davis Senior High School, Da Vinci Charter Academy, Holmes, Harper, and Emerson junior high schools. 1678 is mentored by teachers, team alumni, parents of team members, local college students, and other community members. They frequently start and mentor other robotics teams, even FRC teams like Team 5458 (Digital Minds). They are also involved in other outside projects like helping with University of California, Davis summer camps.

They are known for the record breaking 10 consecutive appearances at the Einstein field during the FIRST Robotics Competition. They have won the championships once and have 58 blue banners, including numerous Impact awards. They consistently rank top 10 or even #1 in EPA according to Statbotics. They often compete at the Sacramento District Event (formerly Sacramento Regional).

## History
Citrus Circuits began in 2004. It was started by Steve Harvey, a math teacher at Da Vinci Charter Academy, in Davis, CA. Originally named EnGen Robotics and later renamed, the team started with 15 students. For their first four years they competed at the Sacramento Regional, only ever being an alliance captain. They worked out of a classroom, then later purchased a shipping container as a workspace.

1678 had their first Regional event win (15-1-0) in 2011 (Logomotion) with a team of 20 students at Sacramento, reaching the quarterfinals of the Curie division (6-6-0). 2012 (Rebound Rumble) began UC Davis' sponsorship of the team, wherein they achieved the same placement in both Sacramento (15-1-0) and Curie (8-4-0), competed in a second Regional event (Central Valley; 9-4-0), and began their formal outreach programs. in 2013 (Ultimate Ascent) they recieved a larger budget, broadened their outreach programs, and reached the Championship semi-finals with a win at the Central Valley Regional (13-7-0) and Curie Division (14-2-0). They pushed farther the following year (Aerial Assist), winning two of the three Regionals they competed in, Inland Empire (19-0-0) and Sacramento (17-1-0). They went on to win the Newton Division and barely lost in the Championship finals.

Despite their impressive record in prior and following years, Citrus Circuits had their first and only Championship win in 2015 (Recycle Rush). During this year they massively grew their program due to major support from their sponsoring school district. They purchased more machines, recieved a larger working room, and began several more robotics classes. To get to their championship win, they won the Central Valley Regional, the Sacramento Regional, and the Silicon Valley Regional. Along the way they recieved a Woodie Flowers Finalist Award, as they would next year as well. They went on to win the Newton Division. Their alliance partners were 118 Robonauts, 1671 Buchanan Bird Brains, and 5012 Gryffingear.

1678 recieved a second classroom in [2016 (Stronghold)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2016-season) and formalized many of their programs. They would win the Central Valley Regional (15-0-1), Sacramento Regional (12-1-1), Silicon Valley Regional (14-0-0), and the Hopper Division (15-1-0), losing in the Semifinals. In [2017 (Steamworks)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2017-season) several milestones were reached, namely 100 team members, a formalized non-profit foundation, and the 5th year in a row at the Einstein field for Championships. They won the Central Valley Regional (13-2-0), the Sacramento Regional (13-2-0), the Las Vegas Regional (13-3-0), and the Newton Division (14-2-0), losing in the Finals. [2018 (Power Up)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2018-season) was the 15th aniversary of team 1678 and their first Chairman's Award (now FIRST Impact Award). They won the Utah Regional (14-1-0), the Sacramento Regional (14-0-0), the Central Valley Regional (17-1-0), and the Newton Division. during [2019 (Destination: Deep Space)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2019-season) they won the Central Valley Regional (15-1-0), Sacramento Regional (14-1-0), Aerospace Valley Regional (18-1-0), and the Carver Division (13-4-0). They recieved their second Chairman's award at the Central Valley Regional.

in [2020](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2020-season), after winning the LA North Regional, the Covid-19 Pandemic would cease all competition for the season. 1678 would nevertheless win a third Chairman's award at the canceled St. Louis Regional event. They continued work online, not in person. in [2021](https://www.citruscircuits.org/2021-season) they continued to compete in the virtual at home events, winning a fourth Chairman's award.

[2022 (Rapid React)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2022-season) would return to normal competition, where 1678 would win the Hueneme Port Regional (17-1-0), Sacramento Regional (16-1-0), Silicon Valley Regional (15-0-0), and the Hopper Division (14-2-0), picking up their fifth Chairman's Award. 2023 (Charged Up) they would win the Hueneme Port Regional (18-2-1), Sacramento Regional (15-0-0), Silicon Valley Regional (13-1-0), and the Galileo Division (15-2-0). [2024 (Crescendo)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2024-season) was their 20th anniversary. They won the Silicon Valley Regional (14-2-1), East Bay Regional (13-1-0), and Archimedes Division (13-2-0). They lost the Sacramento Regional (13-5-0) for the first time in over a decade but in Silicon Valley won a FIRST Impact award.

[2025 (REEFSCAPE)](https://www.citruscircuits.org/history/2025-season) broke the 10 year Einstein streak when they lost in Round 4 of the Daly Division (11-3-0). They would win the Pinnacles Regional (14-3-0), where they would win their 7th Impact/Chairman's award, the Sacramento Regional (16-1-0), and the Contra Costa Regional (16-1-0). Despite the loss they managed to still place highly in other metrics like EPA.

2026 (Rebuilt) is the current season as of writing. They have won an Impact Award at both the Half Moon Bay District Event and the California Northern District Championship (CANCMP). They won the Half Moon Bay (17-0-0) and Sacramento (17-0-0) District Events. They lost in the finals of the CANCMP (13-6-0) to the alliance of 6238 Popcorn Penguins, 1323 MadTown Robotics, and 254 the Cheesy Poofs.
